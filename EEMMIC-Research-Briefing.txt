EEMMIC ENERGY BRIEF
Applying the EMMIC Framework to Pakistan's Energy Sector
================================================================================

WHAT EEMMIC IS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EEMMIC is not another solar/EPC company. It is a platform that connects
energy buyers, EPC contractors, developers, investors, and regulators
through one structured process: Evaluation -> Management -> Marketplace
-> Investment. EEMMIC does not build or own generation itself
— it evaluates, coordinates, and channels deals between the companies
listed below.


REAL COMPANIES IN PAKISTAN'S ENERGY SECTOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

REGULATORS / MARKET INFRASTRUCTURE (not vendors — the rules EEMMIC works inside)
- NEPRA        — sets tariffs, licenses, net-billing/net-metering rules
- CPPA-G       — central power purchasing agency, market operator
- NTDC         — national transmission & dispatch
- DISCOs       — IESCO, LESCO, GEPCO, FESCO, MEPCO, PESCO (grid interconnection)

EPC / SOLAR COMPANIES (real, checkable)
- Reon Energy    — Pakistan's largest C&I solar EPC; 500+MW installed,
                    7,500 telecom-tower sites, 90MWh storage; clients incl.
                    Unilever, Fauji Cement, Kohinoor Textile Mills, Abbott
- Nizam Energy   — founded 2012; 5,000+ projects, 500MWp+ delivered;
                    offers EPC, PPA, and BESS/hybrid-inverter distribution
- Premier Energy — founded 2014 (Lahore, part of UK-based Premier Group);
                    AEDB-authorized net-metering vendor; partnered with
                    State Bank of Pakistan for solar financing/installments
- SkyElectric    — smart-solar specialist; hybrid inverter + AI-managed
                    battery storage (SmartFlow) with 24/7 remote monitoring
                    via its own Network Operations Center
- Pantera Energy — Lahore-based EPC (founded 2013); solar power plants,
                    commercial solar, and residential solar systems
- Alpha Solar    — commercial/residential solar EPC (add real figures once
                    confirmed — not independently verified in this research)

POWER PRODUCERS
- Independent Power Producers (IPPs) — thermal, wind, solar, hydro,
  selling under long-term contracts, mostly to CPPA-G / DISCOs

CUSTOMERS (the buy side)
- Textile mills, cement plants, hospitals, universities, housing
  societies, shopping malls, manufacturing plants

INVESTORS
- Commercial banks, infrastructure funds, private investors,
  international development finance institutions


HOW EEMMIC RUNS EMMIC WITH THESE COMPANIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1) EVALUATION — verifying both sides before anyone bids
   - Buyer side: pull a factory/hospital/mall's electricity bills, load
     profile, and site data to size real demand.
   - Supplier side: verify each EPC's track record before they're allowed
     to bid — e.g. Reon Energy's and Nizam Energy's installed-MW history,
     Premier Energy's AEDB net-metering authorization, SkyElectric's
     monitoring/uptime data, Pantera Energy's completed project list.
   - Regulatory side: confirm the deal structure is legal under NEPRA's
     current net-billing rules and the relevant DISCO's interconnection
     requirements before it goes further.

2) MANAGEMENT — running the project once a supplier is matched
   - EEMMIC tracks the EPC's delivery milestones (design, procurement,
     installation, commissioning) against the timeline it committed to
     at bid stage.
   - Documentation, compliance, and NTDC/DISCO interconnection paperwork
     are tracked centrally instead of chased by phone/email between the
     buyer, the EPC, and the grid company.

3) MARKETPLACE — where the named companies actually compete
   - A buyer posts a verified requirement; Reon Energy, Nizam Energy,
     Premier Energy, SkyElectric, and Pantera Energy (whichever are
     qualified for that job's scale and location) submit competing bids
     — price, timeline, equipment, warranty.
   - Buyer compares bids on the platform instead of collecting quotes
     one WhatsApp message at a time; the win goes to the best-qualified
     bid, not the loudest sales call.

4) INVESTMENT — financing the deal once it's verified
   - Once a buyer and EPC are matched and the deal is evaluated as real
     (not speculative), banks or infrastructure funds review the same
     verified data EEMMIC used at Evaluation stage — reducing their own
     diligence cost.
   - Example: Premier Energy already runs a State Bank of Pakistan
     installment-financing tie-up — this is the kind of arrangement
     EEMMIC's Investment stage is built to standardize and scale across
     every qualified EPC on the platform, not just one.

5) COMPANY — after the project is delivered
   - The completed system (whoever built it — Reon, Nizam, SkyElectric,
     etc.) stays tracked on EEMMIC for ongoing performance monitoring,
     maintenance records, and reporting — turning a one-time install
     into a long-term managed asset.
