Our platform reads buyer bills and load profiles to size real demand, scores suppliers on verified track record, and tracks delivered-system performance — turning a manual, phone-and-email process into a data-backed one.

Grid Interconnection

Interconnection, tracked not chased

We don't operate grid infrastructure — that's NTDC and the DISCOs. What EEMMIC tracks is the paperwork: NEPRA compliance, DISCO interconnection applications, and commissioning sign-off, centralized instead of chased by phone and email.

Compliance Tracking

Deal structures are checked against NEPRA's current net-billing rules before they proceed.

Milestone Tracking
