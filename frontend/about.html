Who We Are

Built to make Pakistan's energy deals trustworthy.

EEMMIC is a platform — not an EPC, not a generation owner. We verify, coordinate, and connect financing for solar and storage deals in Pakistan's B2B and captive power market.

Pakistan-First
Platform Model
Company Overview

A platform, built on a simple premise

Clean energy has to be trustworthy on both sides of the deal to scale in Pakistan's single-buyer market. EEMMIC evaluates buyers and suppliers, coordinates delivery, runs a competitive marketplace, and connects financing — for the B2B and captive net-billing segment real Pakistani EPCs already operate in. We don't build or own generation; we make the deal bankable.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To accelerate Pakistan's shift to solar and storage by making the deal itself trustworthy — verified suppliers, verified buyers, and financing that doesn't have to start diligence from zero.

Our Vision

A B2B and captive solar market where every deal is verified before it's signed — with EEMMIC's evaluation and marketplace model at the core of how that happens.
