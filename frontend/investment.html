How Investment Works

Diligence that starts ahead, not from zero

Investment is the fourth of EEMMIC's four stages. It doesn't replace a financier's own process — it gives that process a verified starting point.

Matched Deal Reviewed

Financiers step in once a buyer and EPC are matched and the deal has already cleared Evaluation.

Reduced Diligence Cost

The load profile, supplier verification, and delivery data EEMMIC already gathered gets reused, not re-collected.

Faster to Close

Shared, verified data shortens the distance between a matched deal and a financed one.
