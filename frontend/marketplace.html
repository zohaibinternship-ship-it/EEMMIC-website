Stage 3 · Marketplace

Where qualified bids compete on merit.

A verified buyer requirement goes live to EEMMIC's pool of vetted EPCs — who compete on price, timeline, equipment, and warranty, not who calls first.

How Marketplace Works

Bidding, after verification — not instead of it

Marketplace is the third of EEMMIC's four stages, and it only opens to requirements and suppliers that already cleared Evaluation.

Verified Requirement Posted

Only requirements that cleared Evaluation are opened for bidding — buyers aren't fielding cold calls from unverified suppliers.

Competing Bids

Qualified EPCs submit bids on price, timeline, equipment, and warranty, all visible side by side.

Best-Qualified Wins

The buyer compares and awards on the platform — the win goes to the strongest bid, not the loudest sales call.

How Bidding Works

From requirement to award

Post a Requirement

A buyer's verified load profile becomes a requirement qualified EPCs can bid on.

Qualified EPCs Bid

Only suppliers that cleared Evaluation can submit a competing bid.

Compare & Award

Bids are compared side by side on price, timeline, equipment, and warranty.
