How It Works

Nothing appears here without clearing four checks

Every listing on this page — illustrative or real — would only go live after this sequence.

Step 1

Evaluation

A buyer's load profile or a supplier's credentials are checked against real technical and financial criteria first.

Step 2

Verification

Documentation, licensing, and track record are confirmed before a profile is eligible to be listed at all.

Step 3

Matching

Verified requirements and verified supply are matched on fit — scale, sector, and location — not who reached out first.

Step 4

Connection

Both sides connect directly on the platform, with EEMMIC's verification already done rather than taken on faith.
