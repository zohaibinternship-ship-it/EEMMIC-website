Stage 2 · Management

Milestones tracked, not chased.

Once a deal is verified, EEMMIC tracks its delivery centrally — design, procurement, installation, and commissioning — instead of buyers chasing updates by phone and email.

How Management Works

One record, from design to commissioning

Management is the second of EEMMIC's four stages. It doesn't replace the EPC's delivery work — it tracks it, centrally, against the timeline committed at bid stage.

Delivery Milestones

Design, procurement, installation, and commissioning are tracked against the timeline the EPC committed to at bid stage.

Interconnection Paperwork

DISCO and NTDC interconnection applications and commissioning sign-off are tracked centrally, not chased case by case.

Shared Documentation

Buyer, EPC, and EEMMIC all see the same record of what's been delivered and what's outstanding.

What Gets Tracked

Delivery, end to end

Design & Procurement

Equipment specification and ordering tracked against the committed scope.

Installation & Commissioning

On-site progress and commissioning sign-off tracked against the bid-stage timeline.

Compliance Sign-Off

NEPRA net-billing and DISCO interconnection paperwork tracked to completion.
